# Close water recordings

All source recordings below are by **felix.blume**, released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `enter-1.wav`, `enter-2.wav`, `flow-1.wav`, `flow-2.wav`, `flow-3.wav`:
  [Underwater recording of aquatic movement, bubbles and drops.](https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/384218/)
- `exit-1.wav`:
  [Water Splash](https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/434978/)

Adapted from the CC0 `submerge.ogg`, `uw_swim.ogg` and `emerge.ogg` excerpts
distributed with [Tidewater](https://github.com/dgreenheck/tidewater), as requested
by the portfolio owner. [Original audio credits](https://github.com/dgreenheck/tidewater/blob/main/public/audio/CREDITS.md).

Processing: quiet close-water excerpts only; mono, 24 kHz / 16-bit PCM,
150 Hz high-pass, 3.2 kHz low-pass, restrained gain, soft attack and tail.
No surf, breaking waves, coastal ambience or speech is included.
Reproducible processing: `tools/prepare_water_audio.py`.
